How Xyrra AI works

Learn the screens. Analyse the insight. Get the alert.

Xyrra watches stocks, crypto, and portfolios, then puts what it finds on screens you can study. When an insight is new, unusual, or relevant to you, an alert brings you back to it.

Start on the dashboard

Your AI Edge Today opens on the strongest insight of the week, then the top stock and crypto performers. Under that, 24-hour, 7-day, and 30-day cards cover stocks, crypto, and investing.

Use it to learn where the AI is finding results before you open a single trade.

Open the live dashboard →

Analyse stock insights

Live Insights lists open stock insights with the current price, performance since the start price, and an upside target. Performance ranks those ideas over the last 24 hours, 7 days, and 30 days. Historical AI shows closed trades, with buy and sell prices, so you can study what already finished.

Open live AI Stocks →

Analyse crypto the same way

Live shows open crypto positions. If nothing is open, it falls back to today’s closed trades. Performance ranks live and closed insights for 24 hours, 7 days, and 30 days. Historical loads closed crypto trades so you can review the full path of an idea.

Open live AI Crypto →

Study portfolio research

AI Portfolios opens on Best Performing. Switch to Trending, AI Recommended, or Lower Risk. Each card shows the year-to-date return, how $1,000 would have grown, the last 30 days, and volatility.

Open live AI Portfolios →

Alerts come from those insights

Turn on smart alerts and Xyrra notifies you when AI spots a potential trade, when it sees unusual activity, and when an insight matches a portfolio you follow. The alert points you back to the screen so you can analyse the idea before you decide.
